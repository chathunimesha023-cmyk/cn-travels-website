Kandy view point

This hillside lookout dishes out a sweeping view of Kandy—lake, Temple of the Tooth, and green hills in one frame. Swing by for sunrise or sunset; it’s an easy tuk-tuk ride or a steep walk up Rajapihilla Mawatha.
